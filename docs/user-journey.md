# The Reknit journey, start to finish

Demo path: **Knee > Patellofemoral pain**, on a phone. About 4 minutes for the set-up and one exercise.

## 1. First time in
| Step | Screen | What the person gets |
|---|---|---|
| Arrive | Welcome | "Move better. Hurt less." and a live camera preview. Try free, or continue with Apple or Google. |
| Consent | Health-data consent | Plain-English use of their data, a medical disclaimer, links to privacy and terms. Withdrawable later. |
| Where | Body area (custom icons) | Knee, Hip, Ankle and foot, Shoulder, Elbow and wrist, Back, with what is ready. |
| What | Condition | The closest match, for example Patellofemoral pain syndrome. |
| Safety | Safety check | Red-flag questions. Any tick stops the set-up and points to NHS 111 / 999. |
| Starting point | Pain 0 to 10 | The baseline their progress is measured against. |
| Plan | Plan ready | Three stages, the exercises, minutes a day, a reminder time, a 14-day trial. |

## 2. Every day (the habit loop)
1. **Today** shows the day's exercises as a checklist with time left, a streak, and the week at a glance. A first-time visitor also gets a three-line "how this works".
2. **Session**: each exercise opens with how to set up the phone, then the camera coach (live form score, spoken cues) or a timer. A quick pain check appears between exercises and stops the session at 7/10 or above.
3. **Finish**: streak, what was done, a pain check-in, then **Tomorrow** (same plan, same time), a **daily reminder that lands in the phone's calendar**, and **where this is going** (Early, Middle, Late, the next stage's exercises, and the two things we look for before suggesting it: pain of 3 or less on the last three check-ins, and training on 8 of the last 14 days).
4. **Progress**: a calendar with a dot on every trained day; tapping a day shows each exercise and its form score. Pain trend, sessions, average form, and a printable report for a clinician.
5. **Next day**: the strip and streak carry on. Miss a day and the streak forgives one rest day; after two missed days a gentle "welcome back" appears. After a flare-up it suggests stepping back a stage.

## Gaps found, and what was done

| Gap a first-time user would hit | Fixed |
|---|---|
| Today said "3 exercises, 9 minutes" without saying which | Today is now a checklist with ticks and minutes left |
| "Remind me at 8:00" saved a time but nothing ever reminded anyone | A daily repeating calendar event with an alert, added in one tap from Today and the end of a session |
| Finishing a session said "See you tomorrow" and nothing else | Streak, tomorrow's plan, the reminder, and the road ahead |
| "Session complete" appeared even when everything was skipped | Honest wording: complete, good work, or "nothing was logged" |
| No sense of what comes after the first stage | "The road ahead" on Plan, and "where this is going" with the criteria for moving up |
| A brand-new user had no orientation | Three-step "how this works" card on the first Today |
| Camera was slow and fussy on iPhone | iPhone engine switched (about 15 s to about 0.1 s) and the distance check relaxed |

## Still open (be upfront about these)
- **Push notifications** (a banner on the lock screen without opening anything) need a server and an Apple developer account. The calendar reminder is the working substitute today.
- **Accounts and cloud sync**: progress lives on the phone. Clearing the browser or switching phones loses it. Sign-in is a placeholder.
- **The plan is the same each day** within a stage; there is no per-day variation (for example strength on alternate days). Stage steps are suggested from pain and training days, not fixed calendar weeks.
- **Physio sign-off** of doses, cues, red-flag questions and the stage-move thresholds is still outstanding.
- Several exercise checks are protocol-based, not validated against filmed footage.
