# Investor demo run-through (about 5 minutes)

**Before you start (do this the evening before, on the phone you will present from)**
1. Open the production site over HTTPS on the phone. Allow the camera when asked. Do one full coached exercise end to end.
2. Add to Home Screen, or open the TestFlight build. Check it opens with no signal on the first page (offline page).
3. Prop the phone at hip height, 2 to 3 metres away, in good light. Practise the line-up once.
4. **Want the streak and calendar to have something to show?** Settings > Presenting Reknit? > Add sample history. It adds five clearly labelled past days (never today), so you can still do today's session live. Remove it afterwards in the same place. Say it is sample data if asked; the app labels it too.
5. Add Reknit to the Home Screen (Safari > Share > Add to Home Screen) so it opens full screen, with no browser bars.
6. Charge the phone. Turn on Do Not Disturb. Turn the volume up so the spoken coach is audible.

**Flow**
1. **Welcome screen.** "Move better. Hurt less." The live preview behind the text is the camera coach.
2. **Try for free.** Accept the health-data consent screen. Point out that it is separate from the terms and can be withdrawn.
3. **Where does it hurt?** Pick Knee, then Patellofemoral pain. Show the safety screening ("tick anything that applies"), then the starting pain score.
4. **Plan ready.** Show the staged plan from the NHS Lanarkshire programme.
5. **Coach.** Start an exercise with the camera. Line up, hold still, watch the live form score and listen to the spoken cues. Finish a hold.
6. **Today and Progress.** Streak, week strip, pain trend. Open Settings to show voice options, data download and delete.

**Say plainly if asked**
- Form scores are estimates from the camera. Several exercise checks are protocol-based and not yet validated against filmed footage.
- Safety wording and thresholds are drafts awaiting physio sign-off.
- Everything is stored on the device today. Accounts, cloud sync, payments and the clinician portal are next.

**If something goes wrong**
- Camera won't start: open `/camera-check` and read the report, or switch that exercise to the timer.
- No signal: the app shell loads offline, but the first visit needs a connection.
- Have a screen recording of a good run on the phone as a backup.
